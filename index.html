🔑 Core Purpose

Emergency Rental and Assistance List: A centralized, easy-to-navigate platform focused on immediate housing aid, financial assistance, and emergency services for people in crisis (e.g., eviction, homelessness, natural disasters, domestic violence, etc.).

✅ Essential Components to Include

  1. Categorized Resource Listings
    • Emergency Rental Assistance Programs (state, local, nonprofit)
    • Shelters and Temporary Housing
    • Utility Assistance Programs
    • Eviction Prevention Services
    • Legal Aid Services
    • Disaster Relief Funds
    • Domestic Violence Support Housing
    • Veteran Assistance Programs
    • Senior and Disability Resources
    • Child and Family Services (e.g., CPS-related housing)
  2. Search & Filter Options
    • Search by ZIP code, city, or state
    • Filter by eligibility (income, family size, veteran status, etc.)
    • Filter by type of emergency (eviction, abuse, disaster, etc.)
  3. Real-Time Updates
    • Listings marked as “Currently Accepting Applications”
    • Status indicators like: Open / Waitlist / Closed
    • Alert system for newly available emergency funds or shelters
  4. Multilingual Support: Content in multiple languages
  5. Mobile-Friendly & Low-Bandwidth Version: Optimized for limited access
  6. Crisis Hotline Integration
    • Quick access buttons for 211, Domestic Violence Hotline, emergency hotlines, suicide prevention
  7. Document Upload & Application Portal: Secure upload and apply features
  8. Live Chat / Chatbot Assistance: Guide users & FAQs
  9. Accessibility Features: Text-to-speech, high contrast mode, keyboard navigation
